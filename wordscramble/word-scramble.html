🔤 How to Play
⏱️
Pick your time & press Start
Choose 30s, 1 min, or a custom duration. The countdown begins the moment you press ▶ Start.
🔀
Unscramble the tiles
Letters are shuffled into tiles. Figure out the original word and type it in the input box.
✍️
Submit or press Enter
Correct → next word loads instantly. Wrong → same word stays, keep trying!
⏭️
Skip to move on
Stuck? Hit Skip to reveal the answer and jump to the next word.
⏸️
Pause freezes everything
Timer stops, input locks, and the hint button is blocked. Resume to continue.
💡
One hint per word
Reveals the first letter + word length. Only available while the game is running and unpaused.
💡 Tip: Unscramble as many words as you can before the timer runs out. Use hints wisely — only one per word!
